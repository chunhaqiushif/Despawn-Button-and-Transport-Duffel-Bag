除“清除尸体按钮”让尸体消失的功能外，补充了两个功能：
1. 根据生成遗物袋时内部的ID卡的阵营情况，让同阵营、中立、敌军的遗物袋显示不同颜色的灯光（绿/黄/红）
2. 如果生成后的10秒没有对其进行任何操作，则会在计时结束后传送至主潜艇的货物生成点
3. 怪物死亡不会生成遗物袋，但是通过“清除尸体按钮”使尸体消失的情况下会生成一个遗物袋

Despawn Button and Transport Duffel Bag (C#)

Uses C# scripts and requires "Lua For Barotrauma" as a dependency.
The code was generated with AI assistance and debugged by me. Please do not use this mod if that bothers you.

In addition to the "Despawn Button" that removes corpses, the following features have been added:
1. Faction-based Lighting: Duffel bags will emit different colored lights based on the faction of the ID card inside when generated (Green for allies, Yellow for neutrals, Red for enemies).
2. Auto-Teleport: If no interaction occurs within 10 seconds after spawning, the duffel bag will automatically teleport to the main submarine's cargo spawnpoint once the timer ends.
3. Monster Corpse Handling: Monsters will not spawn duffel bags upon death. However, removing their corpses via the "Despawn Button" will generate a duffel bag.

An update to ensure compatibility may follow the DLC release (it is rumored that the developers will add support for C# scripting, and adjustments will be made at that time).
